Why "in your browser"? Most online PDF tools upload your file to a server. For contracts, bank statements, IDs and medical records that's a real risk — there have been breaches of exactly these tools. PocketPDF does all the work locally with pdf-lib and pdf.js, so your documents never leave this tab.
