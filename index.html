The idea

Beauty as a
side-effect.

None of these works were drawn. Each is a tiny set of rules — sometimes one line of maths — left to run. The image is not designed; it condenses out of the process, the way frost decides the shape of a window.

That gap fascinates me: between the rule you can hold in your head and the form you could never have predicted from it. It's where weather lives, and cities, and minds. This studio is a small place to stand and watch it happen.
